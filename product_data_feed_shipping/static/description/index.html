Manage shipping data for the leading marketplaces such as Google Merchant Center and Facebook/Instagram Shops

Description

Data feeds usually require shipping data to show this information for customers.

The module add the following fields and models for shipping purposes:

  • Shipping data with country, region, state or province (postal code), shipping label, and a price with a currency. For example: US:NY:Ground:9.99 USD
  • Shipping Label / Group - add and specify the group of your shipping. For example: "oversized", "perishable", or "free shipping".
  • Shipping Weight - special field for setting the shipping weight in lb, oz, g, or kg.
  • Shipping Sizes: Length, Width, and Height in cm, inch.
  • Ships from country - specify the country from which your product will usually be shipped.
  • Transit Time Label / Group - add and define a specific transit time for shipping groups. For example: "Dog food", or "Fragile".
  • Handling Time - specify the maximum and minimum time values for preparing your products for shipping.

This module is part of the Product Data Feed Apps family and it's used for the Google Merchant Center, Facebook Catalog, and Instagram Shop Odoo solutions.

Using
To add a shippiing data, open a product form and on the "Data Feeds" tab fill in fields in the "Shipping" section.
Shipping Groups
To manage shipping groups, go to the menu "Website" - "Configuration" - "Products" - "Data Feeds" - "Shipping Groups".
Shipping Costs
To define shipping costs for countries and regions, go to the menu "Website" - "Configuration" - "Products" - "Data Feeds" - "Shipping Costs" and create one or edit one of existing records.
